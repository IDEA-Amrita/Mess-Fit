# How to bulk on a hostel mess

Gaining weight on a fixed mess menu is mostly about eating *more* of what is already
served, plus a few cheap top-ups. A lean bulk means eating a small surplus of roughly
250–500 kcal per day over your maintenance needs, which usually produces about
0.25–0.5 kg of gain per week. Faster than that and most of the extra weight is fat.

The simplest lever in a mess is portion size. Take an extra katori of rice or dal,
an extra roti, or a second serving of the main sabzi. Carbohydrates from rice, roti,
poha, upma, and idli are inexpensive and calorie-dense, so they do a lot of the work
of hitting a surplus. Pair every meal with a clear protein source — eggs, paneer,
curd, dal, rajma, chana, soya, or chicken if available — so the gained weight is more
muscle than fat.

When the mess protein is thin, add low-cost top-ups: a few boiled eggs, a glass of
milk, roasted chana, or peanuts. A scoop of whey is optional, not required. Training
matters as much as eating: lift progressively (add reps or weight over time) so the
surplus has a reason to become muscle. Weigh yourself weekly under the same conditions
and adjust portions up or down based on the trend, not on a single day.

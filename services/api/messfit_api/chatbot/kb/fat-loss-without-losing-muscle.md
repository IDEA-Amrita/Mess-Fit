# Losing fat without losing muscle

When cutting, the goal is to lose fat while keeping the muscle you have. The biggest
mistakes are dropping calories too aggressively and neglecting protein and training —
all of which cause you to lose muscle along with fat.

Keep the deficit moderate: roughly 300–500 kcal below maintenance usually produces
about 0.25–0.5 kg of loss per week, which is sustainable and muscle-sparing. Protein
should stay high, around 1.8–2.2 g per kg of body weight, because adequate protein in
a deficit is the strongest dietary protector of muscle. Fill the rest of the plate
with high-volume, filling foods — vegetables, dal, curd, fruit — so you stay full on
fewer calories.

Crucially, keep lifting. Resistance training tells the body that the muscle is still
needed, so it preferentially burns fat. Cardio and daily steps help create the deficit
but should not replace strength work. Expect weight to fluctuate day to day from water
and food; judge progress by the weekly trend and how your clothes fit, not a single
reading.

# Hitting protein on a vegetarian or eggetarian diet

Vegetarians can absolutely meet high protein targets; it just takes a little more
planning because plant proteins are less concentrated than meat. The practical move
is to include a protein source at *every* meal rather than relying on one big hit.

Strong vegetarian staples are dairy (milk, curd, paneer), legumes (dal, rajma, chana,
moong, lobia), and soya products (soya chunks, tofu). Combining a legume with a cereal
— rajma-rice, dal-roti, idli-sambar — gives a more complete amino-acid profile across
the day, so you do not need to engineer "complete proteins" at every single meal.
Across a full day, variety takes care of it.

Eggetarians have it easier: two to three eggs add 12–18 g of high-quality protein
cheaply. If you struggle to reach your target from food alone, a plant or whey
isolate shake closes the gap. Aim for a protein source roughly the size of your palm
or a full katori of legumes at main meals, and add curd or milk as the accompaniment.

The mess doesn't hand you a nutrition label, and you're not going to pull out a food scale at the counter. So how do you know if you're eating enough — or too much? Your hand is a calibrated measuring tool you carry everywhere.

## The hand method

Your hand scales with your body, which makes it a surprisingly good portion guide:

- **Palm** = a protein portion (~20–30 g protein): one palm of paneer, chicken, or two eggs' worth.
- **Cupped hand** = a carb portion (~1 katori of rice, ~30 g carbs).
- **Fist** = a vegetable portion; aim for one to two per meal.
- **Thumb** = a fat portion (~1 spoon of ghee/oil, a small handful of nuts ≈ two thumbs).

A balanced mess plate for most students: one to two palms of protein, one to two cupped hands of carbs, a fist or two of veg, and a thumb of fats.

## Translating mess servings

Mess food comes in katoris, spoons, ladles, and pieces. Rough anchors:

- **1 katori dal** ≈ 7–9 g protein, mostly from the lentils.
- **1 katori rice** ≈ 30 g carbs.
- **1 roti** ≈ 15 g carbs + a little protein.
- **1 katori sabzi** varies — paneer/chana sabzis carry real protein; potato/gourd sabzis are mostly carbs and water.
- **1 glass milk** ≈ 8 g protein, 12 g carbs.

You don't need precision. You need to stop *guessing wildly*.

## Adjusting by goal

- **Bulking?** Add a cupped hand of carbs and an extra palm of protein.
- **Cutting?** Keep the protein palms, cut a cupped hand of rice, and fill the gap with fist-fulls of veg so the plate still feels full.
- **Maintaining?** Keep portions steady and let the scale confirm.

> **Hostel context:** Mess servers often give you carbs by default — a mountain of rice, one thin ladle of dal. Flip it. Politely ask for "dal zyada, chawal kam" (more dal, less rice) and take the protein sabzi first before it runs out. The students who hit their protein are the ones who show up on time and ask for seconds of the right thing.

## A quick self-check

After a meal, glance at what you ate: did you get at least one palm of protein? If not, that's your gap — fix it with a top-up (eggs, curd, milk) rather than overhauling the meal.

## Quick FAQ

**My hands are small — does this still work?** Yes, that's the point: your hand scales with your body, so it gives *you*-sized portions automatically.

**What about liquids like dal and sambar?** Estimate by katori, not hand — one katori of dal is your anchor (~7–9 g protein).

**Is eyeballing accurate enough?** It won't match a food scale, but it's far better than guessing, and consistency over weeks (confirmed by the scale) is what actually matters.

## Action items

1. Calibrate: look at your palm, cupped hand, fist, and thumb once so you remember them.
2. Build each mess plate as 1–2 palms protein + 1–2 cupped hands carbs + veg.
3. Ask for more dal/protein sabzi and less default rice.
4. Use a top-up to close any protein gap after the meal.
5. Confirm portions are right by tracking your weight trend over two weeks.
